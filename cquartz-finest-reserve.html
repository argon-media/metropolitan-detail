Why CQuartz Finest Reserve

What you get at the top of the line.

Extreme gloss, real depth.

Finest Reserve builds an incredibly dense, glossy surface that brings out the depth and clarity in your paint, so the car reads better than new. This is the deepest, wettest finish CarPro makes, and it is the reason people step up to it.

Impervious protection, real stain resistance.

The coating is engineered to shrug off oxidation and the things that actually stain a finish: water spots, bird droppings, and bug splatter. It stands between your paint and the environment, and it holds that line for years.

Built to last 3-5 years.

This is a thick, dense coating built on a hybrid nanotech blend, which is why it outlasts waxes, sealants, and lighter coatings. With proper maintenance it protects your finish for 3-5 years.

Slick, hydrophobic, easy to keep clean.

The surface is genuinely slick, so water, dirt, and grime bead up and roll right off. Your car stays cleaner between washes and cleans up fast when you do wash it, even through a wet Redmond winter.

The Process

How we get to a perfect surface.

A coating only looks as good as the paint under it, and this one is close to permanent, so the prep is where the work is.

Imperfect Finish

Where we start. Swirl marks, tar, brake dust, dirt, and bonded contaminants sitting in and on the paint.

FAQ

Questions about Finest Reserve.

How long does Finest Reserve last?

3-5 years with proper maintenance, thanks to its density and thickness.

How is it different from Professional and SiC?

SiC is the entry coating at about a year. Professional is a nano-ceramic hybrid at 2-3 years. Finest Reserve is the flagship, a quartz-infused epoxy nanotech coating at 3-5 years, infrared cured for the hardest, glossiest finish we offer.

What is infrared curing?

After the coating goes on, we cure the panels with infrared heat, which hardens the coating fully for maximum hardness and gloss instead of just letting it set.

Does it stop rock chips?

No. Even the flagship coating is for gloss and easy cleaning, not impact. For chip protection you want paint protection film, and plenty of people do both.

Is paint correction included?

A light correction is, so the coating goes on corrected paint. If you want swirls fully removed, we can do a fuller correction, quoted after we see the car.

How do I take care of it?

Hand wash with a coating-safe soap, and skip wash-and-wax products, they gum it up.

How long will you have my car?

Plan on leaving it with us. The prep is thorough and the coating is infrared cured and needs time. Grab a free loaner and go about your day.
