FAQ

CQuartz SiC questions.

How long does CQuartz SiC last?

About a year with proper maintenance.

How is it different from Professional and Finest Reserve?

It is the entry tier. One coat, one year. Professional lasts 2 to 3 years and Finest Reserve 3 to 5, with more involved processes.

Does it stop rock chips?

No. It is a chemical layer for gloss and easy cleaning. For impact protection you want paint protection film.

Do I need paint correction first?

Light polishing is included. If you want swirls fully removed, correction can be added, priced after inspection.

How do I maintain it?

A two bucket hand wash with a coating-safe detergent, and skip wash-and-wax products.
