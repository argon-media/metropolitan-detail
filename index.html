Core Services

Four ways we protect a finish.

STEK Black Label Certified

Paint Protection Film

STEK Black Label Certified installers are hand-selected and trained directly by STEK for flawless, factory-level installs, a distinction only a small number of shops nationwide have earned.

Custom, near seamless installs that keep clarity and gloss. A self healing topcoat takes light marks out with heat, and the hydrophobic finish makes washing easier.

CarPro CQuartz Reserve

Ceramic Coatings

A ceramic coating bonds to your paint for lasting gloss and easy maintenance. We correct the swirls and scratches first, so the finish looks its best before the coating ever goes on.

We also offer ceramic coating for wheels, glass, and plastics, giving your whole vehicle the same lasting protection.

Window Tint

Premium STEK ceramic tint that keeps your cabin cooler, cuts glare, and blocks 99 percent of UV rays. Better comfort on every drive, and lasting protection for your skin and your interior.

Paint Correction

Bring back the depth and clarity your paint had when it was new. Through careful multi stage polishing, we remove swirls and scratches at the source instead of masking them, so the shine is real and it lasts.
