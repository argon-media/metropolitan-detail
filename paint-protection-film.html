What Is PPF

A clear shield over your paint.

Paint protection film is a clear urethane layer applied over your factory paint. It takes the rock chips, road debris, and light scratches your finish would otherwise take, then absorbs the impact so the paint underneath stays untouched.

We install STEK DYNOshield, which adds a hydrophobic, self healing topcoat. Light swirls fade with heat, water and grime rinse off easily, and the film stays clear without yellowing. Cut and applied custom to each panel, it protects without changing how your car looks.

FAQ

Common PPF questions.

What is STEK DYNOshield?

A clear urethane paint protection film with HYDROphobe technology built into the topcoat. It absorbs rock chips and road debris, self heals light scratches, and repels water and dirt, all without changing how your paint looks.

How long does it last?

Our DYNOshield installations are backed by a 10 year warranty. With normal care the film holds its clarity and gloss for years.

Will it yellow?

No. Film from decades ago yellowed and hazed. DYNOshield is engineered for clarity and stain resistance, so it holds your paint's original color.

Does it really heal scratches?

Light swirls and wash marks heal as the topcoat reflows, and heat speeds it up, so a sunny day does the work. It will not heal a puncture from a rock strike.

Why does a new car need it?

New cars are painted with water borne paint, which is more delicate than paint from decades ago. The most clearcoat your car will ever have is the day you take delivery, so protecting it early means you are not thinning it later with repeated polishing.

My car isn't new. Can I still get PPF?

In most cases yes. Existing chips under the film can show as small bubbles, so if your car has some miles on it we will look it over first and tell you honestly whether it is a good candidate.

Can it be removed later?

Yes. On original factory paint, DYNOshield can be removed without damaging the finish underneath.

Can I get matte or a different color?

Yes. STEK film is available in matte, through DYNOmatte, and in customizable color options, through STEK Fashion Films. Both carry the same protection as DYNOshield.
