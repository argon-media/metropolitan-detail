Additional Exterior Services

When a polish is not enough.

Paint Correction

A multi stage polishing process that removes swirl marks, light scratches, and oxidation at the source rather than hiding them. It restores real clarity and depth to the paint, and it is the right step before any ceramic coating goes on.

Hard Water Spot Removal

Mineral deposits will etch into your clear coat if they sit long enough. We remove them safely and bring the finish back without damaging the protective layers underneath. Worth handling before it becomes permanent.

Overspray Removal

Paint overspray and industrial fallout are a real risk with how much construction is going on around the Eastside. We safely lift bonded contaminants off your paint, glass, and trim and return the finish to smooth.

Maintenance

Twice a year suits most cars, and a season change is a natural time for it. If your car is coated or wrapped, the right maintenance polish will extend the life of what you already paid for, which is the whole point of doing it properly.

FAQ

Common exterior questions.

Which of the three services do I need?

It depends on what is on your paint now. Bare paint gets Essential Exterior. A coated car gets the Ceramic Maintenance Polish. A car with film gets the PPF Maintenance Polish. Not sure? Tell us what you had done and we will point you to the right one.

What does decontamination actually do?

It removes bonded contaminants that a wash cannot touch. Iron particles from brake dust, road tar, and industrial fallout embed in your clear coat. An iron remover, a tar remover, and a clay pad pull them out, which is why the paint feels like glass afterward.

Will this remove my swirls and scratches?

A single stage polish improves the finish, but it will not remove real defects. For that you want paint correction, which is a multi stage process priced after we see the car.

Will the polish strip my ceramic coating?

Not with the Ceramic Maintenance Polish. It uses a non-abrasive product designed to work with a coating rather than cut through it.

Can you polish paint protection film?

Yes. STEK makes a polishing compound formulated for film, which removes oxidation and light marring and restores gloss. Matte film included.

How long does the sealant last?

Three to six months, depending on how the car is driven and stored. If you want years rather than months, look at a ceramic coating.

My car has overspray or heavy moss. Can you help?

Yes, but it takes real labor, so we quote it after an inspection rather than guessing.

Do I need paint correction before a ceramic coating?

Almost always. A coating locks in whatever is underneath it, so any swirls left behind stay there.

How long does an exterior detail take?

Most are a full day. A free loaner keeps you moving, based on availability.
