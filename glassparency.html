A Bond, Not a Coating on Top

Glassparency: a hydrophobic coating for your glass.

You've probably tried a rain repellent that wipes on and washes off within weeks. Glassparency is different. Instead of sitting on top of the glass, it chemically bonds with the silica in the glass itself, creating a durable, water-repelling surface that lasts. That bond is what makes rain bead up and roll away as you drive, so you see more of the road in heavy weather. It cuts glare from headlights, streetlights, and the sun to ease eye strain at night, and it keeps your glass cleaner between washes, so bugs, grime, and hard water spots wipe away with far less effort. Applied by our trained team and backed by a 3-year warranty, with simple seasonal maintenance to keep it performing.

Clearer Glass, Safer Driving

Key Benefits of Glassparency Windshield Coating

Better visibility in all weather.

See more clearly on every commute, especially in the rain. Glassparency's advanced formula makes water bead up and roll off, giving you a clearer view and safer drive on the roads of Redmond, Bellevue, and Seattle.

Less glare, safer driving.

Glassparency cuts the glare from headlights, streetlights, and the sun, which reduces eye strain. It makes for a more comfortable drive, and it helps most on night drives and bright days.

Cleaning gets easy.

Bug splatter and grime do not bond to a coated surface the way they do to bare glass, so they wipe right off. The glass stays cleaner longer, and your regular washes go quicker.

Resists hard water spotting.

Hard water can leave stubborn, damaging spots on your glass. Glassparency seals the surface so mineral spotting is easier to remove and less likely to stain, keeping your windows clear.

Repels ice and frost.

Winter mornings get easier. Ice and frost have a harder time bonding to treated glass, so they clear faster and your windshield is ready sooner.

Backed by a 3 year warranty.

Every application is backed by a 3-year warranty, kept at peak performance with simple seasonal maintenance. Long-lasting protection and real peace of mind.

FAQ

Questions about Glassparency.

What is Glassparency?

A hydrophobic coating that bonds into your glass and makes water bead off, cuts glare, and keeps bugs and water spots from sticking.

How long does it last?

It is backed by a 3 year warranty.

Does it really help in the rain?

Yes. Water beads up and rolls off as you drive, so you see better without relying on your wipers as much.

Can you put it on all my glass?

Yes, we can coat the windshield and other glass surfaces.

My glass has hard water spots. Can you still coat it?

It may need a hard water spot removal service first, so the coating bonds to clean glass. We will take a look.

How is this different from a rain repellent I can buy?

Consumer products sit on top and wear off fast. Glassparency bonds into the glass through a multi-step professional application and is warrantied for 3 years.
