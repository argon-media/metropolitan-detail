FAQ

Common window tint questions.

What is the difference between STEK Action and STEK NEX?

NEX is a graphene film with higher heat rejection and a Skin Cancer Foundation recommendation. Action is a carbon film that balances value and performance.

Will tint interfere with my phone or GPS?

No. Both films are non-metal, so they do not block cell or GPS signals.

How much UV does it block?

Action blocks 99 percent of UV. NEX blocks 99.9 percent and is Skin Cancer Foundation recommended.

Is there a legal limit on how dark I can go?

Washington regulates how dark front windows can be. We will walk you through shades that look good and stay compliant.

How long does an install take?

Most installs are same day. A free loaner is available, based on availability.

What is Glassparency?

A hydrophobic coating for your windshield and glass that improves rain visibility, cuts glare, and repels water.
