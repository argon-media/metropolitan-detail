NEX vs Action

Which one should you get?

Both films block UV and cut heat, so you cannot really go wrong. The difference is the material and how much heat you want gone. NEX uses graphene and rejects more heat, and it carries the Skin Cancer Foundation recommendation, so it is the pick if comfort and protection matter most, even in a lighter shade. Action uses carbon and gives you most of the way there at a better price, with that classic look. Tell us how your car sits in the sun and we will steer you right.

FAQ

Questions we get about tint.

What is the difference between STEK Action and STEK NEX?

NEX is our graphene film with the most heat rejection, and it carries the Skin Cancer Foundation recommendation. Action is a carbon film that gives you most of that performance for less. Both are solid.

Will tint mess with my phone or GPS?

No. We do not use metal films, which are the ones that cause that. Ours leave your signal alone.

How much UV does it block?

Action blocks 99 percent. NEX blocks 99.9 percent and is Skin Cancer Foundation recommended.

Is there a legal limit on how dark I can go?

Washington has rules on how dark your front windows can be. We will walk you through shades that look good and stay legal.

How long does it take?

Most installs are same day. Grab a free loaner and go about your day if you need to.

My new tint looks a little cloudy. Is something wrong?

Nope, that is normal. Tint goes on wet, so you might see a little haze or a few water pockets for a couple of days while it cures. Keep the windows up for five days and it clears up on its own.

What is Glassparency?

A hydrophobic coating for your glass that makes rain bead off, cuts glare, and keeps bugs and water spots from sticking.
