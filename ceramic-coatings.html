The Basics

What a coating does, and what it does not.

A ceramic coating is a liquid nano-ceramic layer that bonds to your clearcoat and hardens into a semi-permanent surface. It gives you deep gloss, strong water beading, UV protection, and a finish that dirt and grime struggle to stick to, which makes every wash easier.

What it does not do is stop rock chips. A coating is a chemical layer, not a physical barrier, so for impact protection you want paint protection film. The two work well together, and plenty of our clients do both.

An honest note on "9H hardness"

A lot of shops advertise "9H hardness." That number comes from a pencil test and means very little on a car. Judge a coating by gloss, durability, and how it is prepped and applied. That is where the real difference is.

Before the Coating

A coating locks in whatever is under it.

If your paint has swirls and scratches, a coating will seal them in rather than hide them. That is why correction comes first. Most shops fill swirls with silicones and waxes to hide them for a while. We remove them, through multiple stages of polishing, so the coating goes on a finish that is actually corrected. Correction is charged by the hour on top of a coating, and we recommend bringing the car by for an evaluation.

FAQ

Common ceramic coating questions.

How long does a ceramic coating last?

Depends on the tier. CQuartz SiC is about a year, Professional 2 to 3 years, and Finest Reserve 3 to 5 years, with proper maintenance.

Will it stop rock chips?

No. A coating is a chemical layer for gloss and easy cleaning, not a physical barrier. For impact protection you want paint protection film. Many clients do both.

Do I need paint correction first?

Usually. A coating seals in whatever is underneath, so any swirls left behind stay there. Light correction is included, and fuller correction is available.

What does "9H hardness" actually mean?

Very little. It comes from a pencil test that does not reflect real-world performance. Judge a coating by gloss, durability, and prep.

How do I maintain a coated car?

A two bucket hand wash with a coating-safe detergent, and a chemical decontamination two to four times a year. Skip wash-and-wax products, which clog the coating.

Can I coat more than the paint?

Yes. We coat wheels and calipers, plastic trim, and glass, each with a coating built for that surface.

How long does it take?

Plan to leave the car. Coatings need cure time, and Finest Reserve is infrared cured. A free loaner keeps you moving.
