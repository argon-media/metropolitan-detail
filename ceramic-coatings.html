A Flawless Finish That Holds

Paint Correction: The Foundation of Every Coating

This is what truly sets us apart. Professional Paint Correction removes scratches and swirls from your paint using the world's best polishes and techniques, not a shortcut. Most detailers use low-grade polishes that fill swirls with silicones and waxes to hide them. We remove them. Every ceramic coating package includes a light paint correction to prep and refine the finish before sealing. If you want to remove deeper swirls and scratches, we can take the paint further through multiple stages of machine polishing. Because that deeper work is so involved, it's charged by the hour on top of your coating, so the paint is fully corrected before it's sealed and protected.

With 30 years of experience and the same standard of prep required to properly bond our CarPro coatings, we recommend bringing your vehicle by for an evaluation first, so we can assess the paint and give you an accurate quote.

FAQ

Questions we get about coatings.

How long does a ceramic coating last?

Depends which one. SiC is about a year, Professional 2-3, and Finest Reserve 3-5, as long as you keep up with washing.

Will it protect against rock chips?

No, and anyone who tells you it will is stretching it. A coating is about shine and easy cleaning. If you want protection from chips, that is paint protection film. A lot of people do both.

Do I need paint correction first?

Yes. A coating locks in whatever is underneath, so we polish the paint first. If it has real swirls you want gone, we can do a fuller correction.

How long will you have my car?

Plan on leaving it with us, since coatings need time to cure and Finest Reserve gets cured with infrared. Grab a free loaner and go about your day.
