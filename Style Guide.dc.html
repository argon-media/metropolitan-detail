06 · Layout & motion

Space and restraint

1280px
Max content width on a 12-column grid, with comfortable gutters.
96–128px
Vertical rhythm between sections. Negative space carries the design.
0–2px
Corner radius. Sharp on cards and sections, 2px on buttons and inputs.
Scroll reveals
Sections fade and slide up gently as they enter view. Smooth, not bouncy. Every section on this page used it.
07 · House rules

Non-negotiables

No em-dashes
Use periods, commas, or restructure. Em-dashes appear nowhere in copy.
No hype words
Avoid premier, elite, world-class, unmatched, industry-leading. Plain, confident copy.
Real only
Real photos and real reviews. No invented stats, no fabricated testimonials.
