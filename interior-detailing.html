FAQ

Questions about interior detailing.

What is included?

A foam bath and 2 bucket hand wash, shampooed carpets and fabric seats, cleaned and conditioned leather, hand cleaned panels, dash, console, mats, and headliner, a final vacuum, and windows cleaned inside and out.

Why does an interior detail include a wash?

Because we are not cleaning your interior while the outside is dirty. The wash is part of the service, at no extra charge.

How often should I book one?

Once or twice a year for most cars, depending on how you use it.

Do you remove pet hair?

Yes, as an add-on. It takes real labor, so we price it after seeing the car.

Can you get a smell out of my car?

Usually. We treat the source of organic odors and deodorize the cabin. It is an add-on, priced after inspection.

What if my interior is really bad?

Tell us. Heavily soiled interiors take more labor, and we will quote it honestly after an inspection rather than surprise you.

Can I book interior and exterior together?

Yes, and it is a common choice. Both services include a wash, and we do not wash the car twice, so it is more efficient combined. Ask us when you book.

How long does it take?

Most interior details are a full day. A free loaner keeps you moving, based on availability.

Do you offer a quick wash and vacuum?

Not at the main shop. For regular upkeep, our Metropolitan Detail Express hand wash in Redmond Town Center is exactly that.
