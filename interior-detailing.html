Interior Detail

One thorough service, priced by vehicle.

We start with a foam bath and a gentle two bucket hand wash, then work through the entire interior. Leather is cleaned and conditioned, fabric seats and carpets are shampooed, and door panels, dash, console, and mats are cleaned by hand. We finish with a final vacuum and clean the windows inside and out. Depending on how you use the car, most people book this once or twice a year.

Pet Hair Removal

Odor Removal
Quote based on inspection

Smells can ruin an otherwise great car, especially one that is new to you. We spot treat the source of organic odors and deodorize the cabin. Added to an interior detail, priced after inspection.

Fabric Guard
$250

A hydrophobic coating for carpets and seats. Spills sit on the surface instead of soaking in, which gives you time to blot a mess before it becomes a stain. Worth it on any family car.

Leather Guard
$250

Leather is durable, but it fades under UV and wears where you get in and out. Leather guard protects against fading and leaves the surface slightly slicker, which reduces wear on the bolsters.

FAQ

Common interior questions.

What is included?

A foam bath and two bucket hand wash, shampooed carpets and fabric seats, cleaned and conditioned leather, hand cleaned panels, dash, console, and mats, a final vacuum, and windows cleaned inside and out.

Why does an interior detail include a wash?

Because we are not cleaning your interior while the outside is dirty. The wash is part of the service, at no extra charge.

How often should I book one?

Once or twice a year for most cars, depending on how you use it and what condition it is in.

Do you remove pet hair?

Yes, as an add-on. It takes real labor, so we price it after seeing the car.

Can you get a smell out of my car?

Usually. We treat the source of organic odors and deodorize the cabin. It is an add-on, priced after inspection.

What if my interior is really bad?

Tell us. Heavily soiled interiors take more labor, and we will quote it honestly after an inspection rather than surprise you.

Can I book interior and exterior together?

Yes, and it is a common choice. Both services include a wash, and we do not wash the car twice, so it is more efficient when combined. Ask us when you book.

How long does it take?

Most interior details are a full day. A free loaner keeps you moving, based on availability.

Do you offer a quick wash and vacuum?

Not at the main shop. For regular upkeep, our Metropolitan Detail Express hand wash in Redmond Town Center is exactly that. Visit MD Express →
