About

Twenty years protecting the Eastside's cars.

Metropolitan Detail has spent two decades on new car protection in Redmond, serving drivers across Bellevue and Seattle. Quality over quantity, and we stand behind what leaves the studio.

Our Story

Built by car people, for car people.

Metropolitan Detail started in 2006 when two childhood friends who loved cars saw a gap in the quality of auto detailing and set out to fix it. One side of the shop was built on paint correction and ceramic coatings, the other on custom paint protection film.

That same standard runs the shop today, from a facility built for the work, with a crew trained from the ground up. This year marks our 20th anniversary.
