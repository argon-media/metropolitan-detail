Two Ways To Use It

Change the finish, or protect the one you have.

DYNOmatte installation

Turn gloss into satin

If you like the matte look but do not want to commit to a repaint or a vinyl wrap, DYNOmatte gives you a factory grade satin finish and protects the paint underneath at the same time. It is fully reversible, and your original paint comes out from under it exactly as it went in, which matters when it comes time to sell. For a lot of our clients in Bellevue and Redmond this is the appeal. You get the transformation without touching the paint or the resale value.

Protect factory matte paint

Matte paint is genuinely difficult to own. You cannot polish it, you cannot wax it, and one careless wash can leave a shiny patch that will not come out. DYNOmatte protects it properly, and because the film's texture matches factory matte, we can cover just the front of the car with no visible line where the film ends and your paint begins. That is not something you can do with most films, and it is why matte owners come to us.

FAQ

Common matte questions.

Can I put matte film over glossy paint?

Yes, and it is one of the main reasons people choose it. You get a satin finish without a repaint, and your original paint stays untouched underneath.

Is it reversible?

Yes. The film comes off and your factory paint is exactly as it was. That is the difference between this and a matte repaint, which is permanent.

Can I do just the front of my matte car?

Yes. DYNOmatte's texture matches factory matte, so there is no visible line where the film ends and the paint begins. Most films cannot do this.

How do I wash a matte car?

Hand wash only. No wax, no polish, no automatic car washes. The good news is that DYNOmatte's hydrophobic topcoat makes it far easier to look after than bare matte paint.

Does it self heal like the gloss film?

Yes. Light swirls and wash marks heal with heat, which is something matte paint cannot do on its own.

Will it look patchy or uneven?

Not when it is installed properly. The finish is consistent across the film, which is exactly why partial coverage works on a factory matte car.

Does it protect against rock chips?

Yes. This is real paint protection film, so it absorbs impacts the same way DYNOshield does.

How long does it last?

Our installations are backed by a 10 year warranty.

Do you serve Bellevue and Seattle?

Yes. We are in Redmond at 8539 152nd Ave NE, and we work with drivers across Bellevue, Kirkland, Seattle, and the wider Eastside.
