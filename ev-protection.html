Why EVs Are Different

An EV is not just a quiet car.

01
Soft factory paint
Thin, water borne paint that marks easily. It is exactly the kind of finish that benefits most from film and correction.
02
Glass roofs that cook
A full glass roof turns your cabin into a greenhouse. Ceramic tint on the roof glass makes a real difference on a summer drive.
03
Frunk and trunk sills
You load an EV from both ends. Those painted sills take scratches from bags, luggage, and gear. Film handles it.
04
Door handle scuffs
Flush and pop out handles put fingernails and keys right on the paint every time you get in. A small piece of film saves that panel.
05
Delivery condition varies
New EVs arrive with paint defects more often than people expect. We inspect before we protect, and we tell you what we find.
06
Wheels still need help
Less brake dust does not mean clean wheels. A high temp coating keeps them glossy and easy to wash.

FAQ

Common EV questions.

Is EV paint really softer than other cars?

Often, yes. Most EVs use thinner water borne paint that marks more easily, which is exactly why film and correction pay off.

Should I protect a new EV right after delivery?

That is the ideal time. The most clearcoat your car will ever have is the day you take delivery. Protecting it early means you are not polishing away that clearcoat later.

Do you tint the glass roof?

Yes. Ceramic tint on a glass roof makes a noticeable difference in cabin heat, and it is one of the most requested things EV owners ask us for.

Which EVs do you work on?

Tesla, Rivian, Lucid, Porsche, BMW, Mercedes, Audi, and more. If it is electric, bring it in.

Do you do PPF on the Cybertruck's stainless steel?

Yes. Stainless cannot be repainted, so film is the right way to protect it against scratches and marring.

Can I combine PPF, coating, and tint?

Yes, and most EV owners do. The order matters, so we map it out for your car.

Do you offer a loaner car?

Yes, free and based on availability. Drop off and keep your day moving.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes. Book online or call (425) 233-6068.
